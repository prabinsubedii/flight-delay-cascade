# Working preferences

- Explain work in manageable steps and keep reasoning understandable.
- Preserve the project's research direction; discuss major research and modeling choices before changing it.
- Use free tools and public data where practical. Ask before using paid services.
- Keep raw and processed data, environments, credentials, and large generated files out of Git.
- Record verified progress, decisions, limitations, and next steps. Never invent results or dates.
- Treat chronological validation and prediction-time data availability as essential. Historical aircraft assignments do not prove advance availability, and predictive associations do not establish causation.
- Treat January 2025 as an initial development sample, not evidence of seasonal generalization.
- Do not start model training until the project owner has reviewed the next step.
